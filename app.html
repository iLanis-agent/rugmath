Rug math

Size the rug to the room, keep the dining chairs on it, pass the front-legs rule. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Labeled norms: 45 cm bare-floor border, 60 cm chair pull-out, standard sizes 120x180 to 300x400 cm. Decorating norms, not law - your room wins.

Room fit

Room size and the bare-floor border become the largest standard rug that fits.

Dining pull-out

Table size and the pull-out norm become the minimum rug for the chairs.

Sofa rule

Sofa length against rug length - front legs on, or how short it falls.
