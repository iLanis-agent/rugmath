Rug math

The rug that looked generous online arrives and floats like a bathmat between the sofa and the wall. Rug math sizes the rug to the room's bare-floor border, checks the dining chairs stay on it when pulled out, and tests the front-legs rule against the sofa before you order. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Room fit - room size and the bare-floor border become the largest standard rug that fits, with the leftover floor it leaves.
  • Dining pull-out - table size and the chair pull-out norm become the minimum rug so back legs never drop off the edge.
  • Sofa rule - sofa length against rug length: front legs on, or exactly how short the rug falls.

Labeled norms

Bare-floor border defaults to 45 cm (a common living-room choice). Chairs pull out about 60 cm behind the table edge. Standard sizes on file: 120x180, 140x200, 160x230, 170x240, 200x300, 240x340, 300x400 cm. These are decorating norms, not law - your room, your furniture and your eye win.
